# Project structure

The complete project is preserved inside `kenya-maize-ndma-coding-sample.zip` under the root folder `Do_Pham_Kenya_Maize_NDMA_Final_Coding_Sample/`.

```text
README.md
02-analysis/
  python/       Panel preparation, weather construction, audits, and exhibit builders
  stata/        Weather models and graph do-files
  arcgis/       Climate and trade map preparation and rendering
03-output/
  figures/      ArcGIS exports, Stata graphs, PDFs, and PNGs
  results/      Coefficients, phase results, model fit, volatility, and audits
  tables/       Editable LaTeX tables
04-data/
  observed/     NDMA panels and derived weather exposures
  sources/      Archived records, catchment evidence, and weather inputs
  geography/    Boundaries, map inputs, GeoJSON layers, and trade routes
```

No manuscript or research paper is included in the archive.
